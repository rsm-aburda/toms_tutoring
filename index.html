About me

I taught high school math for six years before I started tutoring full time. The thing I noticed in the classroom is that most students who say they're "bad at math" are really missing one or two ideas from a year or two back. Once we find those gaps and fill them, the current material starts to click.

My sessions are calm and patient. We work problems together, and I make sure your student can explain each step back to me before we move on. When I'm not tutoring, I'm usually in the water somewhere between Oceanside and Cardiff.

How it works

  1. Book a time

    Pick a slot that works for you on my calendar. Add a note about the class and what's giving your student trouble.

  2. Meet for your session

    We meet online or in person for an hour. Bring homework, a test that went badly, or just questions.

  3. Keep making progress

    Most students meet once a week. You'll get a short summary after each session so you can see how things are going.
